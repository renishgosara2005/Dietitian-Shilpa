PCOD & PCOS Diet Plan



Introduction: PCOD (Polycystic Ovarian Disease) and PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) are common hormonal disorders affecting women of reproductive age. Diet plays a key role in managing symptoms like irregular periods, weight gain, acne, and insulin resistance. This free diet plan is focused on whole foods, managing insulin levels, and promoting hormonal balance.


Key Points of a PCOD & PCOS Diet:

  • Prioritize Low-Glycemic Foods: Foods that help stabilize blood sugar levels can reduce insulin resistance, a common issue in PCOD & PCOS.

  • Increase Fiber Intake: Fiber-rich foods aid in digestion, improve satiety, and control blood sugar.

  • Fats: Include sources of omega-3 fatty acids and other healthy fats to reduce inflammation.

  • Processed Foods and Sugars: These can exacerbate insulin resistance and hormonal imbalances.

  • Meals: Eating at regular intervals can help with blood sugar regulation.


Weekly Diet Plan

    Day 1:

  • Breakfast: Greek yogurt with chia seeds, berries, and a sprinkle of flaxseeds.

  • Mid-Morning Snack: Handful of almonds and walnuts.

  • Lunch: Quinoa salad with grilled chicken, mixed greens, and olive oil dressing.

  • Evening Snack: Apple slices with a teaspoon of almond butter.

  • Dinner: Grilled salmon with sautéed spinach and sweet potato mash.

    Day 2:

  • Breakfast: Scrambled eggs with spinach, avocado slices, and whole-grain toast.

  • Mid-Morning Snack: Sliced cucumber with hummus.

  • Lunch: Brown rice with tofu stir-fry (carrots, bell peppers, and broccoli).

  • Evening Snack: Handful of sunflower seeds or pumpkin seeds.

  • Dinner: Lentil soup with a side of roasted vegetables (zucchini, carrots, and bell peppers).

    Day 3:

  • Breakfast: Oats cooked with almond milk, topped with flaxseeds, cinnamon, and fresh strawberries.

  • Mid-Morning Snack: A small bowl of mixed berries.

  • Lunch: Grilled chicken breast with a side of mixed vegetables and a quinoa pilaf.

  • Evening Snack: Carrot and celery sticks with guacamole.

  • Dinner: Baked fish (tilapia or cod) with roasted sweet potatoes and green beans.

    Day 4:

  • Breakfast: Smoothie made with spinach, banana, almond milk, and chia seeds.

  • Mid-Morning Snack: A handful of pistachios or mixed nuts.

  • Lunch: Buckwheat noodles with tofu, stir-fried veggies, and a soy-ginger dressing.

  • Evening Snack: Pear slices with a teaspoon of peanut butter.

  • Dinner: Turkey or veggie burger on a whole grain bun with a side of salad (spinach, arugula, and walnuts).

    Day 5:

  • Breakfast: Chia seed pudding made with coconut milk, topped with raspberries and almonds.

  • Mid-Morning Snack: A small apple with sunflower seeds.

  • Lunch: Black bean salad with avocado, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and olive oil.

  • Evening Snack: Small bowl of mixed nuts and seeds.

  • Dinner: Grilled chicken or paneer with a side of roasted Brussels sprouts and quinoa.

    Day 6:

  • Breakfast: Scrambled tofu with mixed veggies and whole grain toast.

  • Mid-Morning Snack: A smoothie made with almond milk, flaxseeds, and blueberries.

  • Lunch: Quinoa bowl with avocado, chickpeas, roasted sweet potato, and tahini dressing.

  • Evening Snack: Handful of roasted almonds.

  • Dinner: Zucchini noodles with tomato-basil sauce and a side of steamed broccoli.

    Day 7:

  • Breakfast: Avocado toast on whole-grain bread with a boiled egg.

  • Mid-Morning Snack: A cup of mixed fruit (kiwi, apple, and orange).

  • Lunch: Baked falafel with mixed green salad and tahini dressing.

  • Evening Snack: A small handful of chia seeds soaked in almond milk with a sprinkle of cinnamon.

  • Dinner: Grilled shrimp or paneer with sautéed spinach and quinoa.
