IBS & Constipation Diet Plan

Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) and constipation can be managed effectively through a well-balanced diet focused on improving gut health. This diet plan focuses on consuming fiber-rich foods, staying hydrated, and avoiding trigger foods that may cause discomfort. Here’s a sample diet plan that can be added to your website:

    Guidelines:

  • Hydration: Drink at least 8-10 glasses of water per day to help with bowel movements.
  • Fiber Intake: Aim for 25-30 grams of fiber daily, with a balance between soluble and insoluble fiber.
  • Avoid Gas-Producing Foods: Limit foods that can trigger gas and bloating (beans, carbonated drinks, etc.).
  • Eat Smaller Meals: Smaller, frequent meals are easier on the digestive system.
  • Probiotics: Include probiotic-rich foods like yogurt, kefir, or fermented vegetables to support gut health.
  • Limit Caffeine and Alcohol: These can irritate the gut and lead to constipation.
  • Exercise Regularly: Physical activity helps with regular bowel movements.
