Child Nutrition Diet Plan

Child Nutrition Diet Plan

1. Age Group: 1-3 Years

At this stage, children need nutrient-dense foods to support rapid growth.

    Breakfast:

  • Oatmeal with milk and mashed fruit (banana or apple)
  • A boiled egg or scrambled egg
  • Whole wheat toast with a small amount of butter

    Snack:

  • Sliced fruits (like apple, pear, or mango)
  • A handful of puffed rice or yogurt

    Lunch:

  • Khichdi with vegetables (carrots, peas, spinach)
  • Small pieces of soft paneer or tofu
  • A cup of curd

    Evening Snack:

  • Whole grain crackers with cheese
  • Fresh fruit juice or smoothie

    Dinner:

  • Chapati or rice with dal and soft, well-cooked vegetables
  • A small piece of chicken or fish (for non-vegetarians)
  • Steamed sweet potatoes or carrots

2. Age Group: 4-6 Years

These kids are active and need good energy along with nutrients for growth.

    Breakfast:

  • Whole wheat pancakes with honey and a glass of milk
  • A boiled egg or a bowl of fruit salad

    Snack:

  • Handful of nuts (almonds, walnuts)
  • Fruit like grapes or oranges

    Lunch:

  • Rice or chapati with dal and a vegetable sabzi
  • Grilled chicken or tofu with a side salad
  • Buttermilk or curd

    Evening Snack:

  • Veggie sticks with hummus or yogurt dip
  • A whole wheat sandwich with cheese and cucumber

    Dinner:

  • Chapati or quinoa with paneer curry and mixed vegetables
  • A serving of yogurt or raita
  • Seasonal fruits like papaya or watermelon

3. Age Group: 7-12 Years

Children in this group need foods that support both physical and mental development.

    Breakfast:

  • Multigrain toast with peanut butter and banana slices
  • A glass of milk or a bowl of yogurt with granola

    Snack:

  • Handful of mixed nuts or a granola bar
  • A glass of fresh juice

    Lunch:

  • Brown rice or chapati with dal, vegetables, and salad
  • Grilled chicken or paneer
  • A bowl of yogurt or a glass of buttermilk

    Evening Snack:

  • Homemade smoothie with fruits, milk, and chia seeds
  • Veggie sticks with guacamole

    Dinner:

  • Whole wheat chapati or pasta with a vegetable-rich sauce
  • Grilled fish or chicken (for non-vegetarians)
  • Lentil or chickpea soup

General Tips for Child Nutrition:

  • Encourage a variety of foods to cover all food groups: whole grains, lean proteins, fruits, vegetables, and dairy.
  • Limit sugary snacks and processed foods.
  • Ensure plenty of water intake.
  • Encourage physical activity for overall health.
