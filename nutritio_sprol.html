Nutrition Sprol Diet Plan

Nutrition Sprol Diet Plan

1. Balanced Macronutrients

  • Carbohydrates: Focus on whole grains like oats, quinoa, brown rice, and whole wheat.
  • Proteins: Include plant-based proteins (beans, lentils, tofu) or lean animal proteins (chicken, turkey, fish).
  • Fats: Opt for healthy fats such as avocado, nuts, seeds, and olive oil.

2. Essential Micronutrients

  • Vitamins & Minerals: Eat a variety of colorful vegetables and fruits, rich in vitamins like A, C, and E, and minerals such as iron, calcium, and magnesium.
  • Vitamins & Minerals: Eat a variety of colorful vegetables and fruits, rich in vitamins like A, C, and E, and minerals such as iron, calcium, and magnesium.

3. Meal Structure Suggestions

  • Breakfast: A protein-rich meal such as scrambled eggs or Greek yogurt with fresh fruits and seeds.
  • Lunch: A bowl of mixed vegetables, lean protein, and a whole grain source like quinoa or brown rice.
  • Dinner: A light, balanced meal with lean proteins like grilled fish, and sautéed veggies.
  • Snacks: Opt for fruits, nuts, or a small portion of yogurt between meals.

4. Portion Control

  • Encourage mindful eating by focusing on appropriate portion sizes for each meal.

5. Regular Exercise

  • Highlight the importance of incorporating at least 30 minutes of physical activity daily, such as walking, yoga, or light stretching.
