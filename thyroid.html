Thyroid Diet Plan

Introduction: The thyroid is a crucial gland that regulates metabolism, energy, and overall well-being. An imbalanced thyroid, whether hyperactive (hyperthyroidism) or underactive (hypothyroidism), can lead to a variety of health issues. This diet plan focuses on supporting thyroid function, primarily for those dealing with hypothyroidism.


Dietary Guidelines for Thyroid Health:

    Increase Iodine-rich Foods: Iodine is essential for thyroid hormone production. Include:

  • Seaweed (kelp, nori)
  • Iodized salt (in moderation)
  • Fish and seafood (salmon, shrimp)

    Add Selenium-rich Foods: Selenium helps activate thyroid hormones.

  • Brazil nuts (1-2 per day)
  • Sunflower seeds
  • Chicken, turkey
  • Eggs

    Boost Zinc Intake: Zinc is needed for hormone conversion.

  • Pumpkin seeds
  • Chickpeas
  • Oysters, shellfish

    Include Anti-inflammatory Foods:

  • Fatty fish (salmon, mackerel)
  • Leafy greens (spinach, kale)
  • Berries (blueberries, strawberries)

    Add Fiber for Digestion: Constipation is common in hypothyroidism.

  • Whole grains (brown rice, oats)
  • Vegetables (carrots, broccoli)
  • Fruits (apples, pears)

    Healthy Fats for Hormone Balance:

  • Avocados
  • Olive oil
  • Nuts and seeds

"This meal plan is a general guide and may not be suitable for everyone. Please consult your healthcare provider before making any significant dietary changes."
